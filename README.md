Gilded Shadows — a top-down museum-heist stealth game. You are Wren, a cat burglar slipping through a museum at night. Sneak past patrolling guards, stay out of their vision cones, hide in planters and shadows, steal the Star of Meridian diamond and reach the green exit unseen. Three levels — Gallery, Vault, Rooftop — each with more guards, longer patrols and sharper eyes. Finish a level without ever raising the alarm to earn a GHOST rating.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys. **Sneak:** hold Shift (slower, silent, harder to spot). Running is faster but loud — guards hear your footsteps and come to investigate.
- **Vision cones:** amber when a guard is calm, red when alerted. Walls and crates block sight. Planters hide you unless a guard is very close. Shadow pools slow detection.
- **Detection meter:** fills while a guard sees you, drains when you break line of sight. At full, the guard chases you — break away and hide to cool the alarm down.
- **Objective:** grab the glowing Star of Meridian (plus optional loot gems), then reach the green exit door.
- **Keys:** P or Esc pauses, R restarts, Enter starts.

## Run it

```sh
cd game && python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network — plain static files.
